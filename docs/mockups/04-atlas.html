What coverage does not tell you

Coverage is not support. A retrieved chunk can share vocabulary with the question and still fail to carry the claim.

The stratified audit is small and is not a clinical accuracy estimate. It is reported so the coverage number cannot be read as one.

Reading the bars: overall 46/104 = 44.2% [35.1, 53.8]. Subject questions 22/53 = 41.5% [29.3, 54.9]. Lab questions 20/31 = 64.5% [46.9, 78.9]. Questions naming no subject 4/20 = 20.0% [8.1, 41.6]. No number is shown without its denominator and interval.
